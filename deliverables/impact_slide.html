HealthSathi AI — Your Multilingual AI Health Companion

Bridging Linguistic Barriers & Medical Anxiety Through Responsible, Privacy-First Health Intelligence

🌐 English • हिंदी • मराठी 🛡️ NHS 111 / WHO IMAI Triage 🔒 Privacy by Design

1. Problem & Target Users

  • Language Exclusion: >75% of reputable online medical information is English-only, marginalizing Hindi and Marathi speakers.
  • Anxiety & Misinformation: Unfiltered online searches trigger catastrophic self-diagnosis anxiety and unsafe self-medication.
  • Incomprehensible Labs: Raw numbers and lab ranges induce panic without clinical reference context.
  • Target Audience: Multilingual Indian households, chronic illness self-managers, and first-time diagnostic patients.

2. HealthSathi AI Solution

  • Multilingual Health Companion: Gemini-powered plain-language explanations in English, Hindi, and Marathi with verified offline fallback.
  • Deterministic Red-Flag Escalation: Hardcoded NHS 111 & WHO IMAI emergency ruleset bypasses AI to trigger immediate 112 / 108 alerts.
  • In-Memory Lab Simplifier: Text-based PDF extraction; clarifies reference range variances by lab, age, and pregnancy.
  • Preventive Care Engine: Non-prescriptive medication reminders, appointment tracker, hydration & activity weekly summaries.

3. Phased Adoption Targets

Phase 1: First 100 Users Pilot Target

Supervised university tests, student volunteers, and bilingual Hindi/Marathi usability audits.

Phase 2: 500 Users Growth Target

Campus wellness fairs, peer health educator networks, and localized symptom dialect expansion.

Phase 3: 1,000 Users Scale Target

Partnerships with community health NGOs, outpatient clinic QR educational kiosks, and organic referrals.

5. Responsible Scaling & Safety

  • Privacy Protection: Local SQLite, zero cloud persistence of unconsented medical records, in-memory PDF disposal.
  • Clinical Governance: Deterministic algorithms reviewed against NHS 111 and WHO protocols prior to release.
  • Non-Diagnostic Boundary: Educational companion only; explicitly refuses prescription, dosage, or therapeutic changes.
Clinical Safety Notice: HealthSathi AI is an educational guidance companion and does not provide formal medical diagnoses or prescriptions. For acute emergencies, call 112 / 108 immediately.
