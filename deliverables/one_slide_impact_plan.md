## Detailed Content Breakdown for Slide Creation

### Section 1: Problem & Target Audience
- **The Core Problem**:
  - Over 75% of reputable online medical information is published exclusively in technical English. Non-English speakers face steep linguistic barriers when interpreting diagnostic terms.
  - Search engine "rabbit holes" lead to catastrophic self-diagnosis anxiety or dangerous self-medication.
  - Diagnostic laboratory reports present raw numbers and reference ranges that patients struggle to interpret before seeing a doctor.
- **Target Users**:
  - Multilingual individuals and families in Tier-2/Tier-3 regions seeking clear health guidance in **Hindi (हिंदी)** and **Marathi (मराठी)**.
  - Patients with routine lab test results (CBC, Fasting Blood Sugar, HbA1c, Lipid Profiles) wanting to prepare informed questions for their physician.
  - Individuals managing preventive habits (medication timings, hydration, clinic appointments).

### Section 2: The HealthSathi AI Solution
- **Empathetic Multilingual Companion**: Explains health concepts in conversational everyday analogies across English, Hindi, and Marathi.
- **Deterministic Red-Flag Escalation**: Built on clinician-reviewed protocols (NHS 111 CAS, NICE CKS, WHO IMAI). Bypasses generative AI during acute emergencies to route immediately to emergency hotlines (**112 / 108**).
- **In-Memory Diagnostic Simplifier**: Secure text-based PDF parsing with reference interval context; clearly clarifies that reference ranges vary by lab, age, and pregnancy.
- **Privacy-by-Design**: Local SQLite architecture. Zero data persistence of sensitive medical findings without explicit opt-in.

### Section 3: Phased Adoption Targets (Non-Fabricated Projections)
*Note: All milestones are structured operational targets for pilot testing and scaling, not claimed historical results.*
- **Phase 1 Target: First 100 Users**
  - Supervised usability testing across university campuses and student health centers.
  - Volunteer student cohorts to test language fidelity in Hindi and Marathi.
  - Task completion evaluation on mobile devices.
- **Phase 2 Target: 500 Users**
  - Deployment at community health education desks and college wellness fairs.
  - Direct feedback loops with peer health educators and nursing volunteers.
  - Expansion of common regional dialect synonyms for symptom descriptions.
- **Phase 3 Target: 1,000 Users**
  - Partnerships with grassroots health awareness non-profits and community clinics.
  - Patient education QR codes in outpatient waiting rooms for lab simplifications.
  - Organic peer referrals driven by word-of-mouth accessibility.

### Section 4: Quantitative Success Metrics
1. **Explanation Comprehension**: Target **$\ge$ 85%** of test participants reporting they clearly understood the medical term without auxiliary searching.
2. **Questionnaire Completion Rate**: Target **$\ge$ 80%** completion rate for the 5-step triage questionnaire without drop-off.
3. **Emergency Escalation Reliability**: **100% deterministic success** in identifying critical warning signs (FAST stroke signs, radiating chest pain, severe breathlessness) without AI hallucination.
4. **Accessibility & Parity**: 100% UI and prompt coverage across English, Hindi, and Marathi with zero missing translation keys.
5. **User Experience (CSAT)**: Target **$\ge$ 4.2 / 5.0** usability score on mobile and small-screen layouts.

### Section 5: Responsible Scaling & Safety Framework
- **Privacy-First Telemetry**: Zero analytics trackers. No sale of health data. Local in-memory PDF parsing and ephemeral stream disposal.
- **Clinical Governance**: Regular multidisciplinary review of triage rules by licensed practitioners before adding new symptom categories.
- **Truthful Capabilities**: No claims of background OS SMS or push notifications; transparent disclosure that in-app reminders run within the active browser tab.
- **Educational Non-Diagnostic Boundary**: Strict refusal to diagnose diseases, prescribe pharmaceuticals, calculate doses, or suggest altering doctor-prescribed therapies.
